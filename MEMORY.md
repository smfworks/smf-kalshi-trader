## CFO Competency Research (2026-05-04)

**Purpose:** Develop PhD-level understanding of CFO role, legal obligations, and financial governance

### Key Findings

**1. Four Faces of the CFO (Deloitte Framework):**
- Steward: Protect assets, ensure compliance, manage risk
- Operator: Run efficient finance organization
- Strategist: Shape company direction, M&A, capital markets
- Catalyst: Drive change, cost reduction, process improvement

**2. Legal Liability (SOX + SEC):**
- SOX Section 302: Personal criminal liability for false certifications
- Penalties: Up to $5M fine + 20 years prison
- SEC v. Jensen: Strict liability even without personal misconduct
- Clawback provisions: Recover compensation after restatements
- Control person liability: Presumptively liable for company violations

**3. Fiduciary Duties:**
- Duty of Care: Prudent person standard (not delegable)
- Duty of Loyalty: Company before self (no self-dealing)
- Duty of Obedience: Follow charter and laws
- Breach consequences: Civil damages, criminal penalties, professional revocation

**4. CFTC Regulation:**
- Exclusive jurisdiction over prediction markets
- Event contracts are swaps under CEA
- Political contests defined as "gaming" (prohibited)
- Permitted: Economic indicators, weather, commodities, corporate KPIs
- Kalshi: Fully compliant DCM

**5. Tax Implications:**
- Unclear IRS classification (gambling vs. capital gains)
- Gambling: Ordinary income (up to 37%), limited loss deduction
- Section 1256: Blended rate (~26.8%), favorable loss treatment
- Recommendation: Treat CFTC markets as Section 1256
- Set aside 25-35% of profits for taxes

**6. Treasury Management:**
- 13-week rolling cash flow forecast minimum
- 3-6 months operating expenses in reserves
- Capital allocation discipline
- Monthly variance analysis
